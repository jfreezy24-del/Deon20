STATIC MODEL

The higher-timeframe candle takes the previous candle's low, closes back above it, and the delivery flips. This draws that sequence end to end — sweep, confirmation, targets, invalidation — and keeps the record of every setup it has printed.

How to install Read the source ↗
The setup

Someone's stops filled outside the range, and price didn't stay there.

01

The sweep

Call the previous higher-timeframe candle C1 and the live one C2. A setup exists the moment C2 trades through C1's low or high and comes back inside C1's range.

02

The confirmation

A change in state of delivery says the previous direction has ended. Confirmed mode waits for the closed break; Hybrid surfaces the level earlier.

03

The exit

Targets come from the swept candle itself: 50% is C1's equilibrium, 100% its opposite side. Lines run until they're hit or the setup dies.

When it fails

If price breaks back through the invalidation the setup is marked XC2 and stops being live. The marker and the breached line stay on the chart — a model that quietly deletes its losers is not telling you the truth about itself.

On the chart

Nine layers, each one switchable.

Turn on what you read and turn off the rest. Every layer has its own colour, width and style controls, so the model can sit under an existing chart template instead of fighting it.

  • CISD levels with visible pending / faded / confirmed states
  • Setup history — completed and invalidated setups stay, oldest dropped first
  • Alerts when price breaks the current +CISD or −CISD level

Settings that matter

Three decisions, then leave it alone.

Everything else is cosmetic. These are the inputs that change what the model actually reports.

Installation steps →
InputDefaultEffect
HTF selectionAutoPicks the higher timeframe from your chart. Switch to manual to pin it, e.g. 240.
CISD modeConfirmedConfirmed waits for the closed break. Hybrid surfaces the level earlier, with more noise.
BiasNeutralNeutral takes both sides. Bullish or Bearish restricts the model to setups agreeing with a decided higher-timeframe view.
Max historical setups5How many finished setups stay drawn. Active setups never count toward the limit.
Invalidation lineOnDrawn at the C2 sweep wick — top wick for bearish setups, bottom for bullish.
