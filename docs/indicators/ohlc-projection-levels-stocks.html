Read this first

The statistical model is identical to the crypto edition — manipulation and distribution percentiles, ATR normalisation, recency weighting, conditioning, walk-forward reach and hold rates. That page covers the engine. What follows is only what differs for equities.

What changes

Four differences that matter on an equity chart.

Gaps are part of the sample

An overnight gap through a level counts as reached, exactly as it trades. The Gap direction conditioning mode is worth trying here even though it ships off — an up-gap open is a different animal from a flat one.

Sessions differ between feeds

Index, futures and CFD feeds run different hours than cash equities, so their excursion ratios are comparable but not identical. Pooling smooths that, and ATR normalisation makes it far less damaging.

Keep split adjustment on

TradingView's split adjustment must stay enabled, or decades-old percentages stop meaning anything.

Prefer 1D and above

The 4H option aligns to exchange time and sits awkwardly against a 6.5-hour cash session. Daily, weekly and monthly projections behave properly.

History to borrow

For a young ticker, take someone else's record.

Default behaviour needs none of this — leave the override off and statistics come from your chart symbol's own history, covering any NASDAQ, NYSE, AMEX, OTC or international listing with zero setup.
