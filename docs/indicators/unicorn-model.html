Unicorn Model

A liquidity sweep into a breaker — and the case where a fair value gap overlaps that breaker, giving one zone two independent reasons to matter. It ships with the trade management, not just the marking.

How to installRead the source ↗
The model

Two reasons for one zone.

01

Sweep

Liquidity is taken at an OHLC level, a swing point or a session extreme — you choose which of those count.

02

Breaker

The candle whose structure then breaks becomes the zone. Price returning into it is the entry.

03

Unicorn

When a fair value gap overlaps that breaker, the overlap is the entry. Run Unicorn-only mode to trade nothing else.

Before it activates

Potential setups can be shown while they're still forming, so the zone appears before it is live. Invalidated setups are either ghosted in a faded colour or discarded outright — and invalidation can sit at the model's structural level or at the manipulation extreme, the swing that did the sweeping.

Where liquidity comes from

Three timeframes and four sessions, running at once.

Three configurable sources — 1H, 4H and daily by default — each drawing liquidity from OHLC levels, swing points, or both, mapped automatically or by hand. Session liquidity tracks the highs and lows of Asia, London, New York AM and New York PM on top of that.

  • Bias modes: Neutral, Bullish, Bearish, or Auto from higher-timeframe structure
  • Size filter by bucket, or a custom min–max in points or pips
  • Time filter with up to three named sessions and an explicit timezone

Context layers

What else it puts on the chart.

Higher-timeframe FVGs

Bullish and bearish, auto or manual timeframe, with boxes, labels, midlines, consequent encroachment and optional extension.

HTF candles

Higher-timeframe candles drawn beside the chart with open, high, low and close projection lines and price labels.

SMT divergence

Highs and lows compared against a paired symbol, with a line drawn between the two failures.

Breaker 50%

The midpoint of the breaker, shown separately for bull and bear zones, optionally extended forward.

Implementation note

dynamic_requests is deliberately disabled. Standalone FVG detection carries state across the requested higher-timeframe series, and the one-evaluation-per-bar model is the behaviour this module is built on.
