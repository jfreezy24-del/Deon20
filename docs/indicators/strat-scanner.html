Strat Scanner

An app that scans the market for unresolved Strat forks — the ones where the next bar decides — plus a headless version of the same engine that runs on a schedule and pushes complete trade plans to your phone.

Run it locallyRead the source ↗
The scanner

Potential setups, not finished ones.

Most scanners find sequences that have already completed. This one finds unresolved X-1-? forks and lays out both branches before either happens.

?

The fork

A directional bar followed by an inside bar (2u-1-?): break its high and the move continues, break its low and it reverses. Both are laid out in advance.

↕

Compression

Inside-bar forks are flagged separately — the tightest risk and a genuinely undecided direction, which is what makes them the cleanest of the potential setups.

!

Triggered vs potential

If the forming candle has already taken a trigger out, the signal is marked TRIGGERED — in force — rather than POTENTIAL. No pretending a resolved fork is still pending.

Each signal carriesDetail
ConfidenceA transparent 0–100 score with every contributing factor listed: pattern quality, timeframe continuity, reward-to-risk, trigger-bar close location, volume expansion, in-force bonus.
Why it is validPlain English — the candle sequence, the trapped participants, the continuity picture, the magnitude logic.
LevelsEntry one tick past the trigger, stop the other side of the actionable bar, Target 1 the nearest prior pivot in the trade direction, Target 2 the farther pivot or a 2.5R projection.
TimeframeEvery setup tagged 4H, Daily, Weekly or Monthly. 4H is the lowest scanned, by design.
Algo Alerts mode

A fixed universe, confirmed triggers only.

The second mode drops your watchlist and watches the Mag 7 plus the most liquid US ETFs — SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, SMH, the sector SPDRs, GLD, TLT. No penny stocks, no lottery tickets. Only confirmed triggers on daily, weekly and monthly structure.

  • When a sector ETF fires, it lists same-direction setups in its heavy constituents
  • When a stock fires, it shows the sector ETFs confirming the move
  • Expect a handful of names on a good day and zero on most — it never forces a setup

Weekly crypto ladders

Accumulation, not triggers.

Entries already arrive in real time from the two alerters. What a weekly cadence is good for is the slower question: where the resting bids go, and how much size sits on each one. Every rung is higher-timeframe structure rather than a round number.

  • Size skewed by tier — majors front-load the shallow rungs, high-beta names back-load the deep ones
  • Rungs never more than 35–65% below price; deeper "structure" is a wish, not a bid
  • Assets ordered closest-to-filling first, each with a stance: ACCUMULATE, NEUTRAL or DEFENSIVE
RungWhy it is a level
Prior week lowBelow it the week is 2-down — where weekly stops sit and where failed breakdowns form.
Weekly pivot lowThe last point weekly buyers actually defended; magnitude from above measures down to it.
Prior month lowThe monthly actionable level — below it the monthly sequence is on the back foot.
Monthly pivot lowDeepest structure in the plan. Holding it keeps the monthly sequence intact.
Does it actually work?

The repo carries the tooling to answer that, not just assert it.

A forward-walk outcome resolver, a record of every published signal, historical replay with no look-ahead, an exit-policy grid with walk-forward validation, regime labelling from a benchmark, and a target-quality diagnostic asking whether Target 1 is real structure. Entry refinement uses 60-minute bars for timing only — never for selection.

No API keys

Market data comes from Yahoo Finance's public chart API, with Alpaca for crypto bars. Yahoo has no native 4H interval, so 1H bars are aggregated into 4H buckets anchored to each session's first bar.
